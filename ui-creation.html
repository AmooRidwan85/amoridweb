01 / USER RESEARCH

Meet the user.

A sample persona helps us think about the people using a product.

EXAMPLE PERSONA

Alex Morgan

Digital Product Explorer

Student Urban area
ABOUT

Alex regularly discovers digital services and prefers products that are simple, accessible and easy to understand.

Mobile-first Curious Efficiency-focused
GOALS
  • Find information quickly
  • Complete tasks without confusion
  • Understand what happens after each action
FRUSTRATIONS
  • Unclear navigation
  • Too many unnecessary steps
  • Buttons with confusing labels
NEEDS
  • Clear visual hierarchy
  • Helpful feedback
  • Consistent interface patterns

"I want to know where to go and what to do without having to figure everything out."
